How It Works

1. Create Your Free Business Account

Getting started with RNTR is fast and free. There are no monthly fees - there is only a small transaction fee if you actually book a rental and benefit from RNTR. Join hundreds of other rental providers already growing their business on the RNTR platform.

2. List Your Rental Inventory

Easily upload your rental items, add descriptions, pricing, and availability. You control your terms, rates, and inventory. RNTR gives you the tools to manage your listings with ease — whether you have 10 items or 1,000. The best part? We're here to help! RNTR can bulk upload your items and prices after you sign up.

3. Get Discovered by Thousands of Renters

Your rental store becomes instantly discoverable to thousands of renters using RNTR's app and website. RNTR puts your gear in front of individuals and businesses who are ready to rent — and already searching.

4. Approve Rentals on Your Terms

You decide who rents your inventory and when. RNTR gives you full control to accept or decline rental requests, and customers must agree to your terms and pricing before booking.

5. Get Paid and Grow Your Business

As your listings get more visibility, you get more rentals — and more revenue. RNTR helps rental stores grow online without the hassle of building a website or hiring a marketing team. Plus, we provide your customers with a seamless, easy-to-use mobile app where they can browse, book, and manage rentals anytime, anywhere.
